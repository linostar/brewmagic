BrewMagic Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

This policy covers BrewMagic, a single-player game by linostar for Android and the web.

The short version

BrewMagic does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

What the game stores, and where

To keep your progress, the game saves a small file on your own device (on Android, in the app's private storage), with a backup copy. It holds only game data:

  • your story progress: the day, coins, reputation, stock, upgrades and story choices;
  • your Codex (the recipes and hint words you have learned), the endings you have reached and your Rush Hour best;
  • the difficulty the game has adjusted to your play, and the potions ordered on the last day;
  • your settings: music and sound volumes, Assist mode and Gentle mode.

This file never leaves your device. The game cannot read it from anywhere else, and nobody else can see it. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data) deletes it. In the web version, your browser keeps the same data in its own storage for the game's site, and clearing that site's data in your browser deletes it.

Permissions

The Android app requests no permissions: no internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access.

Children

BrewMagic is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party services. The store or website you get it from (such as Google Play or itch.io) has its own privacy policy for your account and downloads there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact
